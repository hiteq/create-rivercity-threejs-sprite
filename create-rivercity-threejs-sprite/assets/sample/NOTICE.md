# Asset Notice & Provenance

## Provenance and Accuracy Disclaimer
- **Artwork**:
  - `sample-character.png` (128x40, 4 frames: idle-0, idle-1, walk-0, punch-0) and `sample-expanded-character.png` (320x40, 10 frames: idle-0/1, walk-0/1, punch-0/1, kick-0/1, hurt-0, down-0) are original procedural pixel art sprite sheets drawn by `generate-sample-sheet.js` for testing, demonstration, and visual verification.
- **Accuracy Disclaimer**: These assets are **NOT** extracted, dumped, or decoded from any original *Kunio-kun*, *River City Ransom*, or *Downtown Nekketsu Monogatari* ROM or commercial release. **No claim of original game accuracy** is made regarding frame layouts, palettes, timings, or hitboxes.
- **Timings**: Animation durations (e.g., 180ms idle, 133ms 8-tick walk, 67ms/133ms punch/kick, 200ms hurt, 600ms down) are synthetic estimates structured around NES 60Hz tick intervals (e.g. 4 ticks ~67ms, 8 ticks ~133ms, 12 ticks ~200ms) for smooth runtime playback and demonstration.
